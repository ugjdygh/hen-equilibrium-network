# Name and endorsement policy

This is not a trademark licence and claims no registered right. It states what the name may be used for, what it may not, and what happens when someone crosses the line. Its purpose is narrow: keep the **source** of the design identifiable, so that a reader can tell an implementation from the documents.

## 1. What the name is for

`Horizon Equilibrium Network (HEN)` identifies **this repository and the documents in it**. Nothing else. There is no organisation, no foundation, no team, no legal entity behind it.

## 2. Permitted use (nominative, no permission needed)

- citing the design: "based on Horizon Equilibrium Network (HEN)" / "implements the HEN design";
- academic and journalistic reference, with the frozen version cited;
- fork names that clearly distinguish themselves: `X — a fork of HEN`, never `HEN` alone;
- linking to this repository.

## 3. Prohibited use

1. **Assets and venues.** The name or abbreviation may not name a token, coin, fund, exchange, wallet, custody service, lending product, index or trading pair — in any jurisdiction, at any stage.
2. **Implied endorsement.** No implementation, fork, company or individual may describe itself as "official", "endorsed", "approved", "audited by", "the HEN team", "successor to", or may imply that silence from this repository is approval.
3. **Fundraising under the name.** No public sale, private placement, airdrop or grant solicitation may use the name to raise money.
4. **Continuity claims.** No fork may present itself as the continuation of this repository, or as holding its authority. Authority does not transfer; see `GOVERNANCE.md` §7B.
5. **Alteration of the record.** The frozen whitepaper may be quoted, not silently re-edited and republished as the same version.

## 4. If it happens

Escalation, cheapest first — no litigation is contemplated:

1. **Public correction**: a dated statement in this repository naming the use and stating it is not authorised.
2. **Request to rename**: written, with a deadline, and an offer to record the old name in `CHANGELOG.md` so citations survive.
3. **Refusal of any association**: the repository states plainly that it has no relationship with that project, product or person, and stops linking to it.
4. **Nothing further.** No damages claim, no DMCA, no letter from a lawyer. The remedy is disassociation, not punishment.

## 5. Silence means nothing

No reply, no comment, no link, no star, no follow is **not** approval. Approval exists only as a dated statement in this repository saying so. This is written down because "they never objected" is the most common way a research project gets absorbed into someone else's offering.

## 6. Enforcement reality

Unregistered names are enforced by clarity, not by law. What this policy actually buys:

| It buys | It does not buy |
|---|---|
| a public, dated record of what was authorised | an injunction |
| a cheap disassociation step anyone can verify | damages |
| a deterrent against "official" claims | control over code (MIT, irrevocable) |

Anyone determined to use the name badly can still do it. The record makes the misuse visible, and visibility is the whole of the remedy available here.
